iPhone app · FreeStyle Libre 2 (EU)

Glyph.

Your sensor, read directly.

Glyph reads a FreeStyle Libre 2 or Libre 2 Plus sensor straight from your iPhone. Pair once over NFC, then a new reading arrives over Bluetooth about every minute — no LibreLink, no reader, no cloud. It shows them well: a big number with a trend arrow, fourteen days of history, alerts that break through Focus, widgets and a Live Activity. Built for the iPhone Duo first.

Pair your first sensor {{ arrow }} Tour the app

Personal software, not a medical device. Don't make treatment decisions from Glyph alone.

Unfolded on the iPhone Duo: the reading and its chart on one side of the fold, today and the latest readings on the other. A new value lands every few seconds here — about every minute on your arm.

// WHAT GLYPH DOES

One app between your sensor and your phone, and nothing else in the way.

Straight from the sensor

NFC to pair, then Bluetooth about every minute. The readings are decrypted and converted on the phone, and never leave it.

Pairing {{ arrow }}

Now, at a glance

The value in mmol/L or mg/dL, its trend arrow and five-minute change, a three-hour chart, today's time in range and the sensor's days left.

The Now tab {{ arrow }}

Fourteen days of history

Scroll and scrub a 3 to 24-hour window, with average, GMI, time in ranges, variability and an ambulatory glucose profile.

The History tab {{ arrow }}

Alerts that get through

Urgent low, low, high, falling and rising fast, each with its own snooze. A no-data alert fires even when Glyph isn't running.

Alerts {{ arrow }}

Widgets & Live Activity

Home and Lock Screen widgets, and a Live Activity that follows every reading on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

Widgets {{ arrow }}

Apple Health

Writes every reading to Health as blood glucose, with no duplicates, and never reads anything back.

Health {{ arrow }}

// THREE STEPS TO YOUR FIRST READING

01

Start the sensor

Apply a new Libre 2 or Libre 2 Plus and start it with the LibreLink app or a Libre reader. Glyph doesn't start sensors.

What you need {{ arrow }}
›LibreLink ▸ Start new sensor
02

Free up Bluetooth

A sensor talks to one app at a time. Turn off LibreLink's Bluetooth permission so Glyph can take the connection.

Full guide {{ arrow }}
›Settings ▸ Apps ▸ LibreLink ▸ Bluetooth
03

Scan with Glyph

Tap Scan Sensor and hold the top of your iPhone to the sensor. Readings start once its first hour is up.

›Glyph ▸ Sensor ▸ Scan Sensor

// BUILD IT YOURSELF

Open source, MIT, and built with mise and Xcode.

Glyph isn't on the App Store — a CGM can't be. You build it from source and install it on your own iPhone from Xcode or TestFlight.

The Libre 2 protocol and the app logic are Foundation-only Swift packages that build and test on Linux; the app is SwiftUI, generated with XcodeGen. A simulated sensor sends real encrypted frames through the same decoding path, so you can work without one.

LibreKitGlyphCore SwiftUIXcodeGen mise
