Glyph is an iPhone app that reads a FreeStyle Libre 2 or Libre 2 Plus (EU) sensor directly: NFC to pair, then a reading
over Bluetooth about every minute, with no LibreLink, reader or cloud in between. Built for the iPhone Duo first.
